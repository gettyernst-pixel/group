## Definitions used

- **Monthly conversion**: daily × operating_days_per_week × 52/12.
- **Break-even**: first month where cumulative operating profit ≥ initial
  investment (equivalently, cumulative return after investment ≥ 0).
- **ROI**: cumulative operating profit ÷ initial investment. Undefined (shown
  as N/A) when the investment is $0; this is a return multiple, not net of
  the investment — `net_return_after_investment` carries that separately.
